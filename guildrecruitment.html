Frequently Asked Protocols

Is the Guild free to join?

Yes. LVLUP Games is a community-driven project based in the Philippines. Our mission is to provide free technical tools for gamers and developers alike without any paywalls.

How do I earn Guild Ranks?

Ranks are earned by completing tasks on the Quest Board located in the Guild Hall. Each quest submitted and verified by our architects adds reputation points to your operative profile.

What happens to my data?

We prioritize privacy. All recruitment data is used solely for the internal leaderboard and community identification. Check our Privacy Policy for more details.
