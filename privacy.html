Privacy Protocol

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Data Collection

At LVLUP Games, we prioritize player privacy. Most of our tools, including the Loot Spinner and Quest Maker, operate on a Client-Side Only basis. This means the names, questions, and results you input are processed locally in your browser and are never transmitted to our servers.

2. Google AdSense & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google ad and content network privacy policy.

3. Log Files

Like many other Web sites, LVLUP Games makes use of log files. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, type of browser, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks to analyze trends and administer the site.

4. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our privacy policy and agree to its terms. We are committed to maintaining the trust of the Philippine gaming community.
